DocTrust

A reviewer agent that cross-examines a person's whole document set, re-reads anything it is unsure about, and shows the human reviewer the exact regions that agree or disagree. It runs on hosted Muse Spark or Muse Spark Open (open weights) — and never auto-rejects.
